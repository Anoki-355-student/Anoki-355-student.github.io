Pacific Rhododendron

Also known as:

  • Coast Rhododendron
  • California Rhododendron
  • California Rosebay
  • Rhododendron macrophyllum

The Pacific Rhododendron plant may grow to about 24 feet or 8m meters tall, and its leaves may grow to about 8-20cm long. The plant may be found along the west coast of the United States of America, from Southern British Columbia to Northern California. This plant grows best in drier open forests or in the rainshadow areas of mountains.
