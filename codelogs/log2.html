CSS Measuring Units

An absolute unit will consistently remain the same measurement, and is not relative to any other factors. A relative unit is relative to other factors.

Examples of absolute units would include pixels and points or (px and pt). One px is equal to 1/96th of an inch, and one pt is equal to 1/72nd of an inch. Quarter-millimeters, millimeters, and centimeters (or Q, mm, and cm) are also considered absloute units. 1Q is equal to 1/40th of a cm, 1mm is equal to 1/10th of a cm, and 1cm is equal to 37.8 px.

Examples of relative units would include vh and vw. They would be units that are relative to a pages viewport height and width.
