The

Seattle Space Needle

Scroll Down

The Space Needle was opened for business in March 1962, just a bit before the World Fair that would take place from April 21st to October 21st 1962.

About 2,800 cubic yards of concrete and 250 tons of rebar were used to prepare the foundation of the Space Needle. The process took a little over a month to complete.

The core was built upon the foundation, and the legs had followed soon after lasting from June to September of 1961

The fins and ring girders of the Space Needle were added in the fall. The finishing touches of the structure such as the paint and interior were refined during the winter months.
