Coaster

Simple round coaster pattern

Materials

  • Yarn (cotton suggested)
  • Hook (any hook size suggested by yarn weight)
  • Scissors
  • Needle

Instructions

  1. Mr.
  2. 6 sc into the ring. Join with a ss to the top of the first sc. (6 sts)
  3. Ch 1. inc in each st around. Join with a ss to the top of the first sc. (12 sts)
  4. Ch 1. *sc in the next st, inc in the next st* around. Join with a ss to the top of the first sc. (18 sts)
  5. Ch 1. *sc in the next 2 sts, inc in the next st* around. Join with a ss to the top of the first sc. (24 sts)
  6. Ch 1. *sc in the next 3 sts, inc in the next st* around. Join with a ss to the top of the first sc. (30 sts)
  7. Cut the yarn, leaving a long tail. Pull the tail through the last loop on your hook to secure it.
  8. Weave in the ends with a yarn needle.
