Materials

Here is where users will learn about what materials they will need to get started. Before learning the basic techniques, it is important to understand the supplies being used!

Yarn

The type of yarn used for a project can heavily influence many factors of the piece concerning things such as aesthetic appearance or functionality. To learn more about a yarn, when selecting, the label on the yarn should provide more details about the care, fiber content, weight, and more! For now, it is safe to remember those.

As for the yarn types, some of the most popular include:

  • Wool
  • Cotton
  • Acrylic

Wool

Wool yarn is known for its warmth and soft texture. When spun, it is usually somewhat fuzzy! Concerns for this yarn are relevant to its price and availability. Since it is a product of a natural source such as sheep, it tends to be much more expensive to buy when compared to synthetic yarns since the production of those is usually less labor intensive.
