Scarf

Simple scarf pattern

Materials

  • Yarn
  • Hooks
  • Scissors
  • Measuring tape

Instructions

  1. Start with a slip knot and chain until about 6-8 inches in width.
  2. Turn your work and single crochet in the second chain from the hook and in each chain across.
  3. Chain 1 and turn your work. Single crochet in each stitch across. Repeat this step until your scarf reaches your desired length.
  4. Cut the yarn, leaving a long tail. Pull the tail through the last loop on your hook to secure it.
  5. Weave in any loose ends with a yarn needle.
