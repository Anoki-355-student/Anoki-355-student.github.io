Scarf

Simple scarf pattern

Materials

  • Yarn (any type, but acrylic or wool suggested)
  • Hook (size appropriate for your yarn)
  • Scissors
  • Measuring tape
  • Yarn needle (for weaving in ends)

Instructions

  1. Start with a slip knot and ch until about 6-8 inches in width.
  2. Turn your work and sc in the second ch from the hook and in each ch across.
  3. Ch 1 and turn your work. Sc in each st across. Repeat this step until your scarf reaches your desired length.
  4. Cut the yarn, leaving a long tail. Pull the tail through the last loop on your hook to secure it.
  5. Weave in any loose ends with a yarn needle.
