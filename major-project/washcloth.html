Washcloth

Simple washcloth pattern

Materials

  • Yarn (any type, but cotton is recommended for washcloths)
  • Knitting needles (size appropriate for your yarn)
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle (for weaving in ends)

Instructions

  1. Ch 25 sts.
  2. Sc in second st from the hook and each st after. (24 sts)
  3. Sc in every st.
  4. Repeat step 3 until your washcloth is square (approximately 24 rows or so).
  5. Cut the yarn, leaving a long tail. Pull the tail through the last loop on your hook to secure it.
  6. Weave in any loose ends with the yarn needle.
