Hat

Simple hat pattern

Materials

  • Color A Yarn (any yarn, but acrylic or wool is suggested)
  • Color B Yarn (any yarn, but acrylic or wool is suggested)
  • Hook
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle (for weaving in ends)

Instructions

  1. Start with a mr using color A yarn.
  2. Ch 2 (counts as first dc), work 11 dc into the mr. Join with a ss to the top of the ch 2. (12 sts)
  3. Ch 2, dc in the same st, then work 2 dc in each st around. Join with a ss to the top of the ch 2. (24 sts)
  4. Ch 2, dc in the same st, *dc in the next st, then work 2 dc in the next st*; repeat from * to * around. Join with a ss to the top of the ch 2. (36 sts)
  5. Ch 2, dc in the same st, *dc in the next 2 sts, then work 2 dc in the next st*; repeat from * to * around. Join with a ss to the top of the ch 2. (48 sts)
  6. Ch 2, dc in the same st, *dc in the next 3 sts, then work 2 dc in the next st*; repeat from * to * around. Join with a slip stitch to the top of the chain 2. (60 sts)
  7. Rounds 6-10: Ch 2, dc in each st around. Join with a ss to the top of the ch 2 at the end of each round. (60 stitches)
  8. Cut the yarn, leaving a long tail. Pull the tail through the last loop on your hook to secure it.
  9. Weave in the ends with a yarn needle.
