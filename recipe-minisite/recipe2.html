Simple Crepe

Simple crepe recipe!

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 or more milk
  • 1/2 water
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 2 tbps butter melted

Directions

  1. Whisk together eggs, milk, water, and salt. Add and whisk flour and butter.
  2. Pour 1/4 cup of batter into a pan over medium/high heat.
  3. Let cook for 1 to 2 minutes on each side.
  4. Enjoy!
