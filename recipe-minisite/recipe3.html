Hot Chocolate

Hot chocolate recipe!

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1/3 cup boiling water
  • 3 1/2 cups milk
  • 3/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup half and half cream

Directions

  1. Combine sugar, cocoa powder, and salt in a saucepan and whisk in boiling water after.
  2. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in milk.
  4. Remove from heat and add vanilla.
  5. Distribute cream between 4 servings and pour hot chocolate into each.
  6. Enjoy!
